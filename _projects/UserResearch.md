Integrating users into the research process is an integral part of our work to develop technology that is user-centred. The purpose of this guide is to show how we integrate user input into research processes to improve relevance, quality, and usefulness of outcomes, and to present a framework on how to work with us. It outlines general principles for involving users across different stages of a project, with an emphasis on aligning research activities with user needs and ensuring findings can inform decisions and improvements for future research.

### Overview

The guide focuses on the following core principles:

* Involve users early and often
* Use iterative research cycles
* Select methods based on research goals
* Turn findings into actionable outcomes

### Support from the UX Team

The UX team is happy to support research projects at varying stages. We can support projects at the following capacities:

1. **Consulting**: advice on methods, studies, and surveys
2. **Partial Support**: assistance with specific research activities
3. **Research Partnership**: full collaboration throughout the project lifecycle
